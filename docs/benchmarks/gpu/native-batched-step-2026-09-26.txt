Native Q8B full decoder-step geometry/scheduling A/B
====================================================

Device: Apple M4 Max
Checkpoint: local Qwen3-ASR 1.7B, Q8B decoder and Q8B language-model head
Scope: eight independent private KV prefixes, each prefilled with 32 tokens;
full decoder step through all 28 layers and the language-model head. The same
logical next-token position is repeated so cache copies and prefix growth are
outside the timed loop. Every variant's hidden and logits outputs were checked
against the first variant before timing. M4x2 uses two four-lane projection
tiles; M8 uses one eight-lane projection. Attention dispatches are either
serialized or recorded in a concurrent compute encoder with explicit barriers
between dependent stages.
